# Product definition

## Problem

Simple Italian freelancers often pay recurring fees for bookkeeping/tax support even when their fiscal situation is highly standardized. The project aims to automate the repetitive deterministic work and use AI for interaction rather than as the source of fiscal truth.

## First target user

A natural person who:

- is tax-resident in Italy;
- performs freelance software development;
- is considering or uses the regime forfettario;
- has a simple social-security position compatible with the first supported path;
- wants guided opening, invoicing, cash tracking and tax/contribution estimates.

## Product promise

For a supported case, the product should make the next required action obvious and keep a traceable model of why every fiscal number or instruction is shown.

For an unsupported case, the product should clearly stop and explain what requires human/professional review.

## Non-goals for MVP

- replacing every Italian accountant;
- generic tax Q&A;
- aggressive tax optimization;
- acting as an authorized intermediary;
- submitting declarations or payments without an explicit, legally valid integration and user authorization;
- supporting every ATECO code.

## Vertical slice 0.1

1. conversational onboarding;
2. structured taxpayer profile;
3. deterministic eligibility checks;
4. activity classification confirmation;
5. AA9/12 field model;
6. generated review packet;
7. user-facing checklist for submission;
8. audit trail of inputs, rules and outputs.
